Introduction

IN-MOBILITÀ is a project born to transform underused school spaces into vibrant, accessible, and sustainable hubs. Through educational programs, co-design, and urban regeneration, we bring movement to previously stagnant spaces. The goal? To foster real change in daily habits, in the care of common spaces, and in the way we experience school and the city. IN-MOBILITÀ is a collective process that engages, inspires, and builds the future.

Project Goals

Regenerating school spaces, inspiring new habits

We've transformed schools into more livable and sustainable places. We've created dedicated areas for bicycles, light vehicles, and new green spaces, designed in collaboration with students and teachers.

Education for mobility, active citizenship

We have trained students and teachers on topics such as sustainable mobility, citizenship, sustainability, and the care of common spaces. Schools become laboratories for change and participation.

Data that tells the story of change

We collected and analyzed home-school trips to understand how students travel. The data collected helps us design concrete solutions for safer and more sustainable mobility.

Activities

First year

Active citizenship and participation

In groups, we reflected on a provocative question: can students really change the school? A lively and passionate discussion ensued, which helped us rediscover our active role in the school community. Participation is the first step to truly transforming the places we live in.

Community

Community isn't just about living together: it's about relationships, it's about a shared goal. We explored how people come together to build something bigger, sharing their energies, ideas, and responsibilities.

Mobility

We analyzed our travel habits through surveys and peer-to-peer comparisons. A video on Northern European cities inspired us: sustainable mobility isn't a utopia, but a possible model to imagine—and implement—here too.

Space

We looked at school spaces with fresh eyes: how are they experienced? How could they be transformed? Through mind maps and drawings, we began to imagine a more welcoming, green, and functional school.

Co-planning

From ideas, we moved on to projects. Together with professionals, teachers, and municipal representatives, we developed concrete proposals for the regeneration of school spaces: bike parking spaces, green spaces, benches, and meeting areas. The school we want is starting to take shape.

Peer Mobility

We've developed concrete proposals to design and communicate the mobility corner effectively and engagingly. Our goal is to create a dynamic space that fosters meeting, exchange, and collaboration among peers, enhanced by the introduction of a dedicated lesson area.

Trip to Vicenza

This hands-on experience opened new perspectives for us, allowing us to network with design and communication experts. In Vicenza, we gathered valuable ideas and inspiration, transforming our vision into a concrete and tangible project.

Work's presentation

Municipal and provincial authorities participated with interest, attending the inauguration of the mobility corner, which today represents the tangible symbol of a year of commitment, growth, and collective participation.

Second year

Feedback and Relaunch

We took a moment to reflect on the impact of our activities over the past year. What have we learned? What are the opportunities for improvement? This discussion has allowed us to address valuable challenges and inspiration, laying the foundation for a new year of growth and innovation.

Ethical Finance

We explored a fundamental topic: ethical finance, a model that places people and the environment at the center, guiding decisions on how and where to allocate resources. Through reflections and meetings with organizations that practice this approach, we discovered how ethics can transform the way finance is conducted, with a positive and responsible impact on society.p>

Cultural planning

What exactly is a cultural action? We explored various examples, from the more formal to the more informal, and put what we learned into practice by designing our swap party: an event designed to promote reuse, stimulate creativity, and spread sustainability in a concrete and participatory way.

Swap Party

The art of exchange comes to life at our swap party: a unique experience where we shared objects of all kinds, from clothing to stationery to books. A fun and practical way to promote sustainability and conscious consumption, transforming reuse into a moment of socialization and community.

Bike Maintenance

In this hands-on workshop, we discovered how its components work, learned how to repair it, and learned how to maintain it safely. A hands-on experience that taught us how to take care of our vehicle, ensuring optimal performance and safer travel.

What do we say to the institutions?

We organized ourselves into two groups: one prepared a speech describing the site's creation and the progress made over the two years of the project; the other wrote a letter to the institutions, sharing reflections, results, and future proposals.

Gamification

PlaNet is a sustainable training platform designed to be accessible, engaging, and inspiring. It focuses on crucial topics such as sustainable nutrition, the circular economy, sustainable mobility, energy consumption, and diversity and inclusion (D&I). Using gamification as a learning technique, PlaNet makes skill acquisition an interactive and motivating experience, promoting greater awareness and responsibility for a more sustainable and inclusive future.

IIS Silvio Ceccato

The "Silvio Ceccato" School in Montecchio Maggiore is a technical and vocational institute that prepares students to face the challenges of the world of work. It fosters an inclusive and innovative environment that fosters the development of individual skills.

IIS Canova

The IIS Antonio Canova in Vicenza, which includes a technical institute and an art high school, is participating in the In-mobilità project with a CAT class, currently composed of 18 students. The institute values ​​innovation, professional preparation, and a solid foundation.

ITIS Rossi

The "Alessandro Rossi" Technical Institute in Vicenza, near the city center, is a historic institution committed to innovation and engagement with the local community. It offers five majors and participated in the In-mobilità project with a mixed group of second-year student volunteers. Today, the group numbers about ten students.

Liceo Tron-Zanella

The Tron-Zanella-Martini Institute includes all of Schio's high schools: scientific, classical, linguistic, artistic, and human sciences. The class that participated in the project was 4BI, composed of 22 students. This curriculum emphasizes critical analysis and stimulates reflection.

ITET Aulo Ceccato

Two CAT classes participated in the project, one per year, for a total of 39 students. The "Construction, Environment, and Territory" major develops skills in materials, surveying tools, technical software, economic assessments, environmental management, and construction site safety.
